👥 Who can see what

Patient records are separated by doctor. This is enforced by the database, not by the menu, so signing in as someone else is the only way to see another doctor's patients.

  • Admin Every doctor and every report in the clinic
  • Doctor Only their own patients
  • Secretary Only patients of their assigned doctor or doctors
